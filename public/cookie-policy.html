What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They help the website work properly and can provide information to the site owner.

How We Use Cookies

Madden Vehicle Services uses only a minimal number of cookies and similar technologies on this website. We do not sell any information collected through cookies.

Strictly Necessary Cookies

These are required for the website to function and to remember your cookie preference. They cannot be switched off.
