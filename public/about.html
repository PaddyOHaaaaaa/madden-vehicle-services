About Madden Vehicle Services

A family-run business providing honest, reliable servicing and repairs for cars and commercial vehicles across the North West of England.

Who We Are

Madden Vehicle Services is an independent, family-run business specialising in the servicing, maintenance and repair of cars and commercial vehicles. We look after private motorists, sole traders and businesses across the North West of England with everything from a routine oil change to complex fault finding.

Our approach is simple: do the job properly, explain it clearly and charge a fair price. That's why so many of our customers come back to us year after year.

A Family-Run Business

Madden Vehicle Services is a family-run business, and that shapes the way we work. When you bring your car, van or commercial vehicle to us, you're dealing with people who care about their name and their reputation in the community.

Personal Service

You'll speak to the same friendly faces every time, people who know your vehicle and take the time to explain the work it needs.

Trust and Honesty

As a family business, we rely on word of mouth and repeat customers. We'd rather earn your trust than make a quick sale, so we only recommend work your vehicle genuinely needs.

We Treat Your Vehicle Like Our Own

Whether it's the family car or the van your business depends on, we look after it as if it were one of ours.
