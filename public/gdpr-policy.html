GDPR & Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Who We Are

Madden Vehicle Services ("we", "us", "our") is the data controller for personal information collected through this website and when you use our vehicle servicing and repair services. Our address is 1 Tarleton Ave, Atherton, Manchester M46 9QE. You can reach us on 07947 707125 or via our contact page.

Information We Collect

  • Your name and phone number
  • Vehicle details such as registration, make, model and mileage
  • Details of the work you request and service history

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to enquiries and provide quotes
  • To book, carry out and invoice servicing and repair work
  • To send service or MOT reminders, where you have agreed
  • To meet our legal and accounting obligations

Lawful Basis for Processing

Contract

Processing is necessary to provide the services you have asked for.

Consent

Where you have given consent, for example when you opt into service or MOT reminders. You can withdraw consent at any time.

Legal Obligation

We keep certain records, such as invoices, to meet tax and legal requirements.

Legitimate Interests

To run and improve our business in ways you would reasonably expect.

Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal data. We only share it where necessary, for example with parts suppliers, warranty providers or IT service providers who help us run our business, and only under appropriate safeguards.

How Long We Keep Your Data

We keep personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes above. Customer and invoice records are normally kept for six years to meet legal requirements.

Your Rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate data
  • Ask us to delete your data
  • Restrict or object to processing
  • Data portability
  • Withdraw consent at any time

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Cookies

For information on how we use cookies, please see our Cookie Policy.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page. Return to our home page or learn more about us.
